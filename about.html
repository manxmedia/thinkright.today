About Us


At ThinkRight, our mission is to help you accelerate and sustain your development and transformation, be it as a leader, a team, or an organisation. We offer tailormade and customised, courageous, and actionable coaching approaches that raise self-awareness, challenge assumptions, and expose blind spots in our clients to lead them to growth and success.

ThinkRight’s personalised learning approaches help our clients to anchor knowledge, fast-track performance, and foster an environment of endless learning and adaptation. ThinkRight coaching provides you with personal insight and practical tools to enhance your leadership skills, resilience, growth, and success. Whether you choose a stand-alone coaching journey, a team, or business integrated component to learning and development, ThinkRight is committed to promoting a continuous learning environment that will accelerate your performance every step of the way.

Guaranteed Success

I don’t know what exactly you are looking for or if you are at the right place – this only you will know. What I can tell you is that there are no quick fixes to creating the life or the success you dream of. If you want to craft an extraordinary life, then you not only need to have extraordinary committed to your goals but also a willingness to invest the months it will take. ThinkRight Coaching will assist you attaining the skills, the mental and emotional mastery you need to unlock and unleash full potential. We will help you transform your professional life by confronting and displacing limiting beliefs and assumptions, and tailor-making a road map to success that is perfectly suited to you. Now, I need to mention that this journey is about change. You must embrace change in order to success. Yet, change not only doesn’t happen overnight, it is also hard, uncomfortable, and challenging. But rest assured that if you lean into the discomfort, remain committed to the journey, trust the process, and with the right coach to support you; your goals and success is attainable.

I can guarantee you that in our sessions together, if you trust the process, do the inner work that change requires of you, you will discover everything you need to transform your life and be the person that you have always wanted to be – professionally and personally.
