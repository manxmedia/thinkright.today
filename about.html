About Us


At ThinkRight, our mission is to help you accelerate and sustain your development and transformation, be it as a leader, a team, or an organisation. We offer tailormade and customised, courageous, and actionable coaching approaches that raise self-awareness, challenge assumptions, and expose blind spots in our clients to lead them to growth and success.

ThinkRight’s personalised learning approaches help our clients to anchor knowledge, fast-track performance, and foster an environment of endless learning and adaptation. ThinkRight coaching provides you with personal insight and practical tools to enhance your leadership skills, resilience, growth, and success. Whether you choose a stand-alone coaching journey, a team, or business integrated component to learning and development, ThinkRight is committed to promoting a continuous learning environment that will accelerate your performance every step of the way.

Our Main Services

Executive Coaching

Executive and leadership development coaching is a professional development strategy that involves an executive or high-potential employee working with a coach to unlock their full capability, improve their performance, and achieve their goals. The primary aim of executive coaching is to enhance leadership and management performance, through the development of an individual's self-awareness, emotional intelligence, and capacity to influence others for lasting impact.

As your coach we aim to inspire and support you as you work toward realising your full potential and beyond. With our help, you will learn to see yourself more clearly — with compassion and no judgement — and discover the full extent of your capabilities.

Team Coaching

Think Right Team Coaching is a strategic service devised to tackle and resolve team-based challenges within an organisation. By assessing specific concerns and originating customised development programmes, our coaching assist teams to enhance their operational effectiveness and productivity. From promoting a shared vision to implementing targeted development plans.

Think Right Today team coaching helps to bridge communication gaps between managers and their teams, align team members with organisational culture and values, and facilitate opportunities for synergistic collaboration across the organisation.

Life Coaching

ThinkRight Life Coaching is a transformative and personal mastery developmental process that provide a confidential and safe space where you are listened to without judgement or bias. We are primarily focused on practically assisting you to achieve your desired future. With ThinkRight Life Coaching we are committed to seeing the greatness in you even if you do not see it yourself, and we are as determined as you are to helping you achieve your full potential – through the setbacks and the victories.

We partner with you to help you understand what is holding you back and empower you to break past your self-imposed limiting beliefs and self-defeating assumptions. ThinkRight Life Coaching partners with you to confront the self-doubt and fears that are getting in the way of your success.
